    README

    Aurum Revenue Atlas is a single-page allocation console. The frontend combines semantic HTML, CSS custom properties, and vanilla JavaScript. The optimizer sorts offers by value-to-weight ratio, consumes full positions until capacity is exhausted, then applies a fractional take to the final position.
